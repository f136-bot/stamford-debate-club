About Us

We believe thoughtful dialogue is the foundation of leadership.

The Debate Club at Stamford American High School is a new and growing student community focused on helping students develop stronger reasoning, clearer communication, and a more balanced understanding of the world around them.

Our club is designed to provide a space where students can explore current issues, evaluate evidence, and practice persuasive speaking in a respectful environment. We aim to cultivate intellectually curious students who can approach problems from multiple perspectives and make informed decisions with confidence.

  • Build critical thinking and analytical reasoning skills
  • Strengthen public speaking and persuasive communication
  • Explore important school and global issues from multiple perspectives
  • Encourage respectful collaboration and team-based learning
  • Develop leadership through structured debate and discussion
