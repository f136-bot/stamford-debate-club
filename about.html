About the Club

Building thoughtful students through structured dialogue.

Stamford American High School Debate Club is a student-centered initiative created to encourage critical thinking, respectful discussion, and confident public speaking. We believe voices are strongest when grounded in evidence, clarity, and mutual respect.

Mission

Why we exist

The club was founded to provide a constructive environment where students can explore ideas, challenge assumptions, and learn how to argue effectively without losing respect for others. We believe debate is not only about winning, but about developing the ability to think carefully, communicate clearly, and act responsibly.

Through structured practice, students strengthen their understanding of current issues, learn how to support claims with evidence, and develop confidence in expressing themselves in academic and real-world settings.

Values

What we cultivate

  • Analytical thinking and logical reasoning
  • Persuasive and confident communication
  • Respect for different perspectives
  • Evidence-based decision making
  • Leadership through preparation and dialogue
Structure

Two debate tracks, one shared purpose

Chinese Debate Group

This track focuses on Chinese-language argumentation, structured reasoning, and discussion of topics relevant to student life, culture, and community issues. It helps students build fluency, analytical depth, and strong rhetorical communication.

English Debate Group

This track emphasizes formal speaking, evidence-based argumentation, and confident public expression in English. Students refine delivery, support their views with research, and participate in high-level discussion and rebuttal.

Mentorship

Guided by experienced teachers

Chinese Teacher Mentor

Provides guidance in critical reading, structured Chinese argumentation, and discussion strategies in a culturally relevant context.

English Teacher Mentor

Supports speaking development, formal debate preparation, and persuasive communication in English through constructive feedback.
