Activities

Regular practice, consistent growth.

Our club follows a weekly structure that allows students to build skills gradually and become more confident over time. Each session is designed to sharpen reasoning, strengthen delivery, and create a supportive environment for practice.

Format

One group each week

1 Group

Weekly Debate Group

Students meet once per week in a focused group setting to practice argument building, rebuttal, and speaking techniques.

2 Tracks

Chinese & English Debate Tracks

Members participate in either the Chinese Debate Group or the English Debate Group based on their strengths and goals.

8 Sessions

Eight Internal Debate Activities

The club will hold eight internal debate activities throughout the program, giving every member the opportunity to practice, improve, and develop confidence in a classroom-friendly format.

2 Mentors

Instruction and Guidance

One Chinese teacher and one English teacher support the development of students through feedback, structured instruction, and encouragement.

Program Focus

What students will develop

🧠

Evidence-Based Argumentation

Students learn to support claims using logic, examples, and concise reasoning rather than emotion alone.

🎤

Public Speaking Skills

Members improve clarity, pacing, structure, and confidence when presenting their positions in front of others.

🤝

Respectful Discussion

Debate helps students learn how to challenge ideas constructively while maintaining professionalism and respect.
